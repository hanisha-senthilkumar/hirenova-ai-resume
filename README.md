🚀 HireNova — Resume & Job Description Matching Tool

Team Name: MetaMinds

📌 Problem Statement

Students and job seekers often apply for internships and job opportunities without knowing how well their resume matches the requirements of a specific job description. Manually comparing resumes with job descriptions is time-consuming and may cause users to miss important skills, keywords, and qualifications required by employers.

There is a need for an intelligent solution that can automatically analyze a resume against a job description, identify matching and missing skills, and provide a clear understanding of the candidate's suitability for the role.

💡 Proposed Solution

HireNova is an AI-powered web application designed to help students and job seekers evaluate their resume against a specific job description.

The platform allows users to upload or provide their resume and job description. The AI analyzes both inputs, extracts important skills and keywords, identifies matching and missing competencies, and generates an overall Resume–Job Match Score.

Key Capabilities

- 📄 Resume Analysis
- 💼 Job Description Analysis
- 🔍 Skill & Keyword Matching
- ❌ Missing Skill Detection
- 📊 Resume–Job Match Score
- 💡 Resume Improvement Suggestions
- 🎯 Job Relevance Analysis
- 🤖 AI-Powered Recommendations

The platform helps users understand their skill gaps and improve their resumes before applying for internships or job opportunities.
