🚀 Project Overview

Resume & Job Description Matching Tool is an intelligent web-based solution developed to help students and job seekers evaluate the relevance of their resumes against specific job descriptions. The platform automatically analyzes the uploaded resume and job requirements, identifies matching skills and important keywords, detects missing competencies, and generates an overall Resume–Job Match Score. By providing clear insights into skill gaps and resume alignment, the solution enables users to improve their resumes and make more informed career decisions before applying for internships or job opportunities.
